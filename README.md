# Keezen

Hot-seat Keezen for one laptop: 4- or 6-colour board, each colour gets its own hand, cards stay hidden until the next player presses "show my cards".

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site once enabled (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, folder `/`).

House rules in this version:
- All colours of one person form a team: on your turn you pick which colour's hand to play from, and any card may move any of your pawns.
- Passing your entry you choose: into home (exact count) or another lap.
- If none of your colours can play, all your cards are discarded; the other player keeps playing out their hand.
